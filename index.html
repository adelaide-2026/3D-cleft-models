How to use this site:

Tap or click on a card below to open an interactive 3D model for that type of cleft or common cleft procedure. Each page lets you rotate and explore the model in your browser. If you are viewing the page on a compatible mobile phone or tablet with a camera, you can place a life-sized version of the 3D model in the room with you using augmented reality (AR).

This resource is part of the research project "The Use of 3D Printed and Augmented Reality Models as Educational Aids in Cleft Care: A Comparative Study" under Adelaide University. Each card below links to a dedicated interactive model and information page for a specific craniofacial condition or procedure.
